Program

Six months engineered around adoption.

One week of onboarding, two weeks of in-person immersion in Silicon Valley, then six months of mentored execution with milestone tracking. Built for the realities of defense procurement, export control and allied market entry.

PHASE 1 · 1 WEEK

Online onboarding

Align on goals, map your target markets and buyers, and arrive at the residency with the groundwork already done.

PHASE 2 · 2 WEEKS

Silicon Valley residency

Working immersion hosted with SRI International: positioning, curated meetings with primes and investors, workshops, and ecosystem access.

PHASE 3 · 6 MONTHS

Remote execution & mentoring

Expert mentoring, partner and investor follow-ups, and milestone tracking until the pipeline is real.

What you gain

Defense immersion

Positioning work, partner meetings and a high-trust gateway into the Valley's defense ecosystem.

Founder-level execution

Strategy, narrative, GTM, negotiation, export compliance and leadership, taught for defense and dual-use conditions.

Transatlantic and Indo-Pacific bridge

Structured access to primes, defense investors, allied stakeholders, operators, mentors and R&D partners.

Who should attend

DTSA is built for founders and senior leaders who can make strategic decisions in the room and act on them afterwards.

Founder / CEO CTO involved in strategic partnerships Chief Strategy or Business Development lead

The participant must be able to represent the technology and its operational validation. If the attendee cannot make decisions, the program will not deliver its value.

FAQ

What does “battle-tested” mean here?

Operational validation in real-world or combat-like environments — not lab tests or demos.

What stage is required?

We select TRL 6+ teams. Exceptionally, TRL 4–5 companies with completed operational validation are considered.

Can dual-use startups apply?

Yes. DTSA is built for both defense and dual-use technologies.

Which markets does DTSA focus on?

The United States, Europe and the Indo-Pacific allied markets.

How is sensitive information handled?

Share only what is required for evaluation and partnership conversations. Nothing is passed on without your consent.

How do we apply?

Complete the application form on the Apply page. Selection runs on a rolling basis until the cohort is full.

Apply

Apply to Cohort 3.

Check the fit criteria, then send the application. It takes about twenty minutes if your validation evidence is ready.

Open the application form

You are a fit if

You are TRL 6+ and past the prototype stage. Your technology is validated in operational or combat-like environments. You have pilots, users or paying customers. You are preparing for international scale and allied market entry. A decision-maker can commit to the in-person residency.

You are not a fit if

You are at idea or pre-validation stage. You are still building the prototype or MVP. You have lab tests and demos but no field validation. You are focused on local markets only. You cannot commit to a multi-month execution program.

Exceptional teams may still be considered.

What to prepare

Evidence of validation

Where the technology has been deployed, under what conditions, and what it achieved. Share only what you can share.

Traction

Pilots, users, contracts, orders — with the customer type even if the name is confidential.

Scale intent

Which allied markets you are targeting, and what is currently blocking entry.
