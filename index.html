Is DTSA Beast Mode For You?

What “Beast Mode” Means at DTSA

  • •Battle-Tested: Proven performance in real operational or combat-like environments.
  • •Entrepreneur-Led: Founders with ownership, speed, and execution DNA.
  • •Agile-Powered: Rapid iteration, feedback loops, and high-velocity pivots.
  • •Scale-Focused: Built for production, procurement, and allied-market expansion.
  • •Tech-Driven: Deep expertise in AI, autonomy, EW, sensing, comms, robotics, and dual-use innovation.

If your startup meets these criteria and is TRL 6+ with operational validation, you are ready to go Beast Mode with DTSA.

We are looking for visionary teams tackling mission-critical challenges across Air • Land • Sea • Space • Cyber • Autonomy • AI • EW • Sensing • Robotics • Dual-Use Resilience • Bio & MedTech.

You are a fit if your company:

  • Is TRL 6+ and past prototype stage.
  • Validated in operational or combat-like environments (or equivalent high-stress field conditions).
  • Has pilots, users, or paying customers.
  • Is preparing for international scale + allied market entry.
I’m a Fit → Apply Now

You are not a fit if your company:

  • Is in pre-validation, idea-stage, or looking for theory-first acceleration.
  • Is still building the prototype or MVP.
  • Has only lab tests or demos, with no operational or field validation yet.
  • Is focused solely on local markets with no intention or plan for international/allied scale.
  • Not able to commit to the in-person residency and multi-month execution program.

Exceptional Teams May Be Considered — if your startup is building the future of defense and dual-use technology, you are in the right place.

Who Should Attend & Program Fees

Who Should Attend

DTSA is designed for founders and senior leaders who can make strategic decisions and fully engage in the program. The ideal representative is: Founder / CEO, CTO involved in strategic partnerships, or Chief Strategy or Business Development leader.

The participant must be able to represent the technology and its operational validation, engage with investors and primes, and act on strategic decisions during and after the program. If the attendee cannot make decisions, the program won’t deliver its full value.

FAQ
What does “battle-tested” mean for DTSA?
Operational validation in real-world or combat-like environments.
What stage is required?
We are selecting top TRL 6+ teams, and in exceptional cases, we may consider TRL 4–5 companies that have completed operational validation.
When will we receive a decision?
By March 20, 2026.
What is covered and what is not?
For Ukrainian startups, participation is fully covered by the Ukrainian Alumni Fund and other supporting donors. Participants cover only travel and accommodation. For all other applicants: the program fee is $8,250 + travel and accommodation.
Who should attend from the team?
A founder or senior decision-maker (CEO/CTO/Strategy/BD).
Is DTSA focused on execution or learning?
Execution-focused: procurement readiness, market entry, partnerships, and scaling.
Can dual-use startups apply?
Yes. DTSA is built for defense and dual-use technologies.
How is sensitive information handled?
Share only what is required for evaluation and partnership conversations.
What markets does DTSA focus on?
U.S., European, and Indo-Pacific allied markets.
